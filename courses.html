⚡ Analysis of Algorithms

Undergraduate • UNICAP

Asymptotic time and space complexity analysis, Big-O / Big-Omega / Big-Theta notation, recurrence relations (Master Theorem), algorithmic design paradigms (divide-and-conquer, greedy methods, dynamic programming), and graph algorithms.

Interactive Lecture Reveal.js + Warsaw Beamer KaTeX + Simulators

Lecture 5: Complexity of Iterative Algorithms (Part 2)

3-step complexity calculation method, quadratic sorting analysis (Selection Sort & optimized Bubble Sort with triangular sums), best/worst/average cases (Linear Search), $O(n \cdot m)$ nested loops, $O(\sqrt{n})$ primality testing, Two Pointers technique, and 10 solved exercises with interactive live step simulators.

🌐 Systems Architecture

Undergraduate • UNICAP

Architectural models for distributed systems, structured and flat naming, Distributed Hash Tables (DHTs), Domain Name System (DNS), clock synchronization, distributed election algorithms, and microservices fundamentals.

From identifiers to structured hierarchical naming, reverse FQDN resolution, DNS namespace layers (Global, Administrational, Managerial), authoritative comparative analysis, iterative vs. recursive name resolution trade-offs, and multi-tier DNS caching with interactive live Canvas simulators.
