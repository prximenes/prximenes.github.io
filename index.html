👤 About Me

I am a Professor of Computer Science at the Universidade Católica de Pernambuco (UNICAP) and a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at the Informatics Center of Universidade Federal de Pernambuco (CIn-UFPE).

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Computer Science with an emphasis on Networks and Distributed Systems. My research investigates Large Language Models (LLMs) for Automated Root Cause Analysis (RCA) in Network Virtualization, Computer Networks, Task Offloading, Digital Twin Networks, Optimization, Linux Kernel & Fast Packet Processing, and Cloud/Edge Virtualization, with extensive application in Machine Learning and Data Science.
